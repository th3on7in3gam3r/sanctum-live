# Control Room Guide

The Control Room is where pastors and Admins lead live prayer gatherings.

## Before going live

- **Open sanctuary**: Required before congregants can join a waiting session. Enter the sermon leader name for this session.
- **Pre-Prayer Check**: Test microphone, speakers, and network before starting.
- **Start Prayer**: Transitions the session to LIVE. Raised-hand invites only work when LIVE.

## Host microphone

- Your **host microphone** carries sermon audio to the congregation.
- Turn it on when you are ready to speak. Congregants hear you through this mic in Sermon mode.

## Sermon mode vs Discussion PTT

- **Sermon mode** (default): Congregation microphones are locked. Only you and ministers can speak.
- **Discussion PTT**: Open when you want congregants to participate. Each person **taps to speak** and **taps again to stop**. Only one congregant speaks at a time.

## Congregation roster

- Shows everyone connected with mic status icons:
  - Green = speaking live
  - Red = ready for PTT
  - Grey = locked (Sermon mode)
- **Raised hand** and **prayer request** icons appear on names.
- **Press and hold a name** for private prayer — only you and that person hear each other.

## Raised hands

- Congregants tap **Raise Hand** on the join page.
- Names appear in the **Raised Hands** panel.
- Tap **Invite to Pray** when the session is LIVE.
- Tap **Dismiss** (X) to lower the hand.

## Invited speakers

- When you invite someone to pray, they see an **Accept & Pray** banner on the join page.
- They must accept before their microphone is available.
- Invitations only work when the session is LIVE.

## Chat

- Switch to the **Chat** tab to see gathering messages from congregants.

## End session

- Tap **End Prayer Session** when prayer is finished. Everyone sees a summary and the session moves to History.
