# Getting Started with Sanctum Live

Sanctum Live lets your church run live audio prayer gatherings. Pastors and Admins lead from the **Control Room**. Congregants join via a public link — no account required.

## First-time setup

1. Sign in and complete **Onboarding** to create your Sanctum Space (church name, slug, timezone).
2. Open the **Dashboard** — your home base for prayer rooms and settings.
3. Use the **Pastor walkthrough** in the sidebar for a numbered Step 1–17 guide from creating a room to going live.

## Create a prayer room

1. Dashboard → **Create Prayer Room** (or Prayer Rooms → New).
2. Enter a **room name** (e.g. Morning Prayer).
3. Optionally add a **room logo** (public image URL).
4. Review the **permanent join link** (`/join/your-slug`) — it never changes.
5. Tap **Create Prayer Room** to save.

## Share the join link

- From **Room Details** or the Dashboard card, copy the join link.
- Send it by text, email, or bulletin. Congregants open it on phone or computer.
- They enter their name and join as listeners.

## Lead a gathering

1. Open **Control Room** for the prayer room.
2. **Open sanctuary** and enter today's **sermon / prayer leader** name (required).
3. Run **Pre-Prayer Check** to test your microphone.
4. Tap **Start Prayer** when ready.
5. Turn on your **host microphone** to speak.
6. Use **Sermon mode** (congregation muted) or **Discussion PTT** (congregants tap to speak, one at a time).
7. Monitor the **roster**, **raised hands**, and **chat**.
8. **End Prayer Session** when finished.

## Roles

- **Owner / Apostle**: Full access including team invites and billing settings.
- **Admin**: Room operator — can moderate Control Room, PTT, raised hands, and private prayer while the named sermon leader speaks.
- **Sermon leader**: The person named when opening sanctuary; shown to congregants.
- **Congregant**: Joins via public link; listens, raises hand, taps to speak in Discussion PTT.
