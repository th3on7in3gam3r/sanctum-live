# Settings, Team, and Branding

## Settings tabs

### General Info
- Church name, slug, country, timezone, and website.

### Branding & Join Page
- Church logo URL and join-page welcome message.
- Congregants see this branding on the public join page.
- Click **Save Branding** — success may take a moment on slow connections.

### Team (Owner only)
- Invite **Admins** to help operate Control Rooms.
- Admins can moderate rooms, open the floor, manage raised hands, and private prayer.
- Team list supports pagination (25 per page) and removal.

### Prayer Defaults
- Low bandwidth mode, auto-mute settings, and prayer request options.

### Security & Privacy
- Audit notes and delete Sanctum Space.

## Admin vs sermon leader

- **Admin** = room operator in Control Room (moderation, speaking floor, roster).
- **Sermon leader** = the person named when opening sanctuary for that session; displayed to congregants.
- The same person can be both, or an Admin can operate while another person gives the sermon.

## Inviting Admins

1. Settings → **Team** tab.
2. Enter email and send invite.
3. They sign in with Clerk and accept the organization invite.
